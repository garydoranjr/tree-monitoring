Where the RPC thinks the camera was

Each PlanetScope scene is orthorectified with a geometric model (RPC) that was tied to the ground near lake level. Its line of sight is tilted away from the direction the satellite reported, so the model is right at the tie points and wrong by height × tilt above them. Three scales of the same geometry, from real scenes over Barro Colorado Island.

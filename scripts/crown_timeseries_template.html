BCI 50ha: drone vs Planet classification confidence per crown

Drone: per-crown mean probabilities from the crown classifications (phantom to , mavic after), and the training-mask event probability 1 − (1 − pflowering)(1 − pdeciduous); an event is > 0.5. Planet: the epoch-12 temporal Mask R-CNN on scenes between flights; max score is the highest detection score on a clear crown pixel, and covered fraction the share of clear crown pixels under a detection scoring ≥ 0.5 (a Planet event is ≥ 0.25).
